## Data from JustAC

The files in `Data/` are generated by `scripts/import_justac_data.py` from the
WoW Forever data tables of JustAC 6.0.0 (https://github.com/wealdly/JustAC,
GPL-3.0-or-later, Copyright (C) 2024-2026 wealdly):

- `Data/Rotations.lua` - priority lists, rank chains and class abilities
  (`Data/Forever/Rotations.lua`);
- `Data/Defaults.lua` - defensive, pet, group, gap-closer, burst and buff lists
  and spell metadata (`Data/Forever/Defaults.lua`);
- `Data/Interrupts.lua` - interrupt and crowd-control abilities
  (`Data/InterruptAbilities.lua` with the Forever additions);
- `Data/Consumables.lua` - pre-combat consumables, recovery items and healing
  items (`Data/Forever/Consumables.lua`);
- `Data/CCBreakers.lua` - crowd-control breakers (`Data/CCBreakers.lua`).

Only the data is used; Forever One Button contains none of JustAC's code.
`Data/Leveling.lua` (leveling lists, levels 1-59) is written for Forever One
Button and is not imported.
